# Solon open work

The open thinking behind [Solon](https://solonai.org): product principles, architecture,
decisions, and build notes.

Solon turns human overrides in agent-driven workflows into durable, versioned policy. The
product lives at solonai.org. This repository is the public record of how it is designed
and built.

## What lives here

- `product/`: what Solon is, its principles, and what it is not.
- `architecture/`: how the system is put together.
- `decisions/`: dated decisions with context, options, and consequences.
- `build-log/`: curated notes from work sessions.
- `PUBLISHING.md`: the boundary and the gate that decide what may appear here.

## The publishing promise

Everything here is curated, and nothing arrives without a human merge. Every change lands
as a pull request, reviewed and merged by a person. That is the same rule the product
enforces for policy: a human approves every change.

## Status

Living documents, dated and versioned like code. The build log starts at entry 0001. The
history of this repository is the record.
