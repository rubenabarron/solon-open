# Build log 0001: the site positioning batch

Date: 2026-09-30 | Status: published

## What shipped

A positioning pass on solonai.org: a "Why now" section on the homepage and the governance
page, a new hero lede ("Human overrides become AI policy: automatically, auditably, and
only with your sign-off."), a comparison row for legal guardrail platforms, a section
reorder, and structured data additions (a software application schema plus an organization
description).

## How it was verified

- Local: type check, unit tests, static build, an 82-check site audit, and a contrast
  check, all green.
- Visual: 28 screenshots across pages, sizes, and themes, reviewed against an
  anti-pattern checklist.
- Preview deployment checked at 82 checks, then production deployment checked at 84
  checks (the extra two are redirect checks that run only in production).
- Search engines notified: IndexNow accepted all 7 URLs (HTTP 200).

## What we learned

- Edge propagation is real: the first check immediately after a preview deployment saw
  two transient 404s; direct requests returned 200 and the re-run passed. The habit now
  is to wait about a minute before checking a fresh deployment.
- Structure beats volume: reordering the page did more for the story than adding more
  sections would have.
